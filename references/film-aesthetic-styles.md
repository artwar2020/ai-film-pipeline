# 影视流派与年代美学档案库 (Cinematic & TV Style Vault)



🎬 Cinematic & TV Style Vault  
A Complete Reference Guide of Era, Genre & Regional Visual Styles for AI Image Generation

### 📺 TV Styles

1.  1990s Sitcom (Multi-Cam)  
Style of 1990s sitcoms, warm balanced colors, flat even lighting, minimal shadows, clean image, studio set feel, multi-camera staging. Panavision Panaflex or PSR-200 on 35mm film (multi-camera setup) / later transitioned to HD video. 

2.  1990s Teen Drama  
Style of 1990s teen dramas, warm natural tones, soft lighting, slight glow, low contrast, suburban outdoor and indoor sets. 35mm film (Panavision or Arri) or Sony Betacam SP videotape. 

3.  1990s Fantasy TV  
Style of 1990s fantasy TV shows, soft diffused lighting, warm tones, light haze, moderate grain, practical costume and set design. 35mm film or high-end Betacam SP. 

4.  1990s News Broadcast  
Style of 1990s TV news broadcast, neutral color balance, bright even lighting, no shadows, ultra clean image, studio desk environment. Sony Betacam SP or Ikegami broadcast cameras on videotape. 

5.  1980s Soap Opera  
Style of 1980s soap operas, warm saturated tones, soft focus, bright flat lighting, low contrast, indoor studio environments, theatrical blocking. Sony or RCA 1-inch Type C videotape cameras. 

6.  1980s Sci-Fi TV  
Style of 1980s sci-fi television, bold color lighting, low-budget practical effects look, flat lighting, minimal depth, studio-built futuristic sets. 1-inch videotape or 16mm/35mm film (budget dependent). 

7.  2000s Crime Procedural  
Style of 2000s crime procedural TV, cool blue tones, high contrast lighting, sharp highlights, digital clean texture, lab and interrogation room sets. Sony F35 CineAlta or Arri D-21 digital cinema cameras / some 35mm film (e. g.  early CSI). 

8.  2000s Medical Drama  
Style of 2000s medical TV shows, cool sterile colors, bright overhead lighting, reflective surfaces, clean digital look, hospital environments. Sony HDW-F900 or Panasonic VariCam digital HD cameras. 

9.  2000s Reality TV  
Style of 2000s reality TV, boosted saturation, uneven lighting, digital noise, handheld framing, uncontrolled environments. Sony PD150 / VX2000 or Panasonic DVX100 miniDV cameras. 

10.  2000s Police Reality (Cops-style)  
Style of 2000s police reality shows, harsh on-camera lighting, blown highlights, deep shadows, heavy digital noise, raw handheld footage. Handheld Sony PD150 / VX2100 or similar prosumer DV cameras. 

11.  2000s Paranormal Investigation  
Style of 2000s paranormal TV shows, low light, green/blue night vision tones, high noise, handheld camera feel, dark interior locations. Night-vision capable DV cameras (Sony Handycam) + infrared. 

12.  2010s Prestige Drama  
Style of 2010s prestige TV dramas, cinematic color grading, soft shadows, controlled highlights, subtle film grain, high-end production design. Arri Alexa or RED Epic digital cinema cameras. 

13.  2010s Mockumentary  
Style of 2010s mockumentary TV shows, natural colors, practical lighting, slight overexposure, handheld feel, real office environments. Panasonic AF100 or Canon C300 digital cinema cameras (handheld). 

14.  2010s Superhero TV  
Style of 2010s superhero TV series, high contrast lighting, bold color accents, cinematic grading, clean digital texture, stylized sets. Arri Alexa or RED Dragon. 

15.  2010s Nordic Noir TV  
Style of Nordic noir TV series, cold desaturated palette, natural low light, soft contrast, subtle grain, overcast outdoor environments. Arri Alexa or RED digital cinema cameras. 

### 🎬 Film Styles

1.  1960s Spaghetti Western  
Style of 1960s spaghetti western films, warm dusty tones, harsh sunlight, deep shadows, wide framing feel, visible film grain, dry desert atmosphere, practical outdoor sets. Arri 2C or Mitchell cameras on 35mm film. 

2.  1970s Horror Film  
Style of 1970s horror films, muted colors, low lighting, heavy shadows, practical lighting sources, gritty film grain, analog imperfections, claustrophobic interior sets. Arri 16mm (e. g.  Eclair NPR) or 35mm Arri BL. 

3.  1970s Documentary  
Style of 1970s documentary film, natural lighting, slightly faded colors, raw realism, handheld feel, strong film grain, unpolished environment. 16mm Éclair NPR or Arri SR handheld. 

4.  1940s Film Noir  
Style of 1940s film noir, black and white, extreme contrast lighting, sharp shadows, venetian blind light patterns, high grain, studio-built sets, smoky atmosphere. Mitchell BNC or Arri on 35mm black & white film. 

5.  1980s Action Film  
Style of 1980s action films, high contrast, neon accents, hard directional lighting, slight overexposure on highlights, VHS-era film grain, practical explosions feel, gritty urban set design. Panavision or Arri PL on 35mm Kodak 5247/5294 film. 

6.  1980s Sci-Fi Film  
Style of 1980s sci-fi films, practical effects look, cool blue lighting, glowing highlights, fog diffusion, analog film grain, retro-futuristic set design. Panavision or Arri on 35mm film. 

7.  1990s Crime Thriller  
Style of 1990s crime thrillers, desaturated colors, cool blue-green tones, low-key lighting, realistic shadows, subtle film grain, handheld camera feel, real-world locations. 35mm Arri or Panavision (often with anamorphic lenses). 

8.  2000s Hollywood Drama  
Style of early 2000s drama films, soft cinematic lighting, balanced color grading, natural skin tones, shallow depth of field, clean film grain, realistic interior sets. Panavision or Arri on 35mm film / early digital (Sony F900). 

9.  2000s Epic Fantasy  
Style of 2000s epic fantasy films, dramatic lighting, rich color grading, golden highlights, atmospheric haze, cinematic depth, large-scale set feel. Arri or Panavision on 35mm film (often Super 35). 

10.  Modern Indie Film  
Style of modern indie films, natural lighting, soft contrast, muted tones, handheld realism, minimal grading, authentic locations, subtle grain. RED, Arri Alexa Mini, or Canon C300 digital.

4.  1970s Soviet Sci-Fi  
Style of 1970s Soviet sci-fi films, washed-out colors, soft diffused lighting, low saturation, grainy film texture, minimalist futuristic sets, philosophical tone. Soviet Konvas or imported Arri on 35mm. 

5.  1980s Latin American Telenovela  
Style of 1980s Latin American telenovelas, warm saturated tones, flat studio lighting, soft focus, low contrast, indoor set feel, theatrical staging. 1-inch videotape studio cameras. 

6.  1990s Japanese TV Drama  
Style of 1990s Japanese TV dramas, soft pastel colors, diffused lighting, gentle highlights, low grain, clean indoor environments, emotional close-ups. Sony Betacam or early HD video. 

7.  Late 1970s French New Wave  
Style of late 1970s French New Wave films, natural lighting, slightly desaturated tones, light grain, loose framing, real-world locations, spontaneous feel. Éclair NPR 16mm or Arri 35mm. 

8.  Dogme 95 Inspired  
Style of Dogme 95-inspired films, natural colors, no artificial lighting, handheld camera feel, raw digital noise, real locations, unpolished realism. Consumer DV cameras (Canon XL1, Sony PD150) or 16mm. 

9.  1980s Industrial Training Film  
Style of 1980s industrial training films, flat color reproduction, fluorescent lighting, minimal shadows, slight VHS grain, sterile office or factory environments. U-matic or Betacam videotape. 

10.  1990s European Art TV  
Style of 1990s European art television, muted colors, soft contrast, slow lighting transitions, light film grain, minimalistic set design, contemplative mood. 16mm or 35mm film / early digital video.
